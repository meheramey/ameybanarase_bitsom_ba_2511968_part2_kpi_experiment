# Part 2: KPI Framework, Business Experiment Analysis & Decision Recommendation

## 📝 Task 1: Business Problem Statement
The company has introduced a new onboarding and activation campaign to improve user conversion and early engagement. As a Business Analyst, the key decision is to determine whether this new treatment experience should be launched to all users globally or rejected. This decision directly impacts the product team, customer success teams, and the overall user base. While the primary goal is to maximize the Paid Conversion Rate, we must closely monitor critical risks such as an inflation in refund rates and customer support tickets, which could degrade revenue quality and increase operational costs. Before making a final rollout recommendation, statistically significant evidence from A/B testing and a thorough net-revenue impact analysis are strictly required.

## 🎯 Task 2: North Star Metric Selection
Our selected North Star Metric for this experiment is:
**Paid Conversion Rate = (Total Users Converted to Paid) / (Total Users in Group)**

### Justification & Business Context
* **Connection to Business Growth:** For a subscription-based digital product company, direct revenue-generating conversion is the most critical growth driver.
* **Supporting Metrics vs. North Star:** Other metrics like Landing page visit rate, Trial start rate, and Onboarding completion rate are merely supporting metrics (leading indicators). If a user completes onboarding but does not pay, the business does not grow financially. Therefore, the final monetizable event is our true success metric.
* **The Blind Optimization Risk:** Blindly chasing this metric might lead to aggressive or misleading onboarding promises. While this could artificially boost short-term conversions, it risks spiking Refund Rates and Customer Support Tickets if users feel misled or find the product confusing. This is why our Guardrail metrics are essential.
